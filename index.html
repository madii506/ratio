the rule

Every tweet is a coin.

Paste a tweet. It becomes a coin on Pons, paired to the stock it is about. The tweet is the coin's name, face and lore. The author is on the payroll whether they know it or not.

I

The tweet. One exact tweet. Its author, its text, its views. The coin cannot change it.

II

The ratio. The coin's market cap against the tweet's views. A tweet with a million views and a $10,000 coin is at one cent a view. That number is the headline, not the market cap.

III

The fee. Every trade pays 1%. 0.5% waits in the author's vault. 0.2% goes to whoever launched it. 0.3% is Pons.

IV

The claim. The author replies to their own tweet with a code. The vault sends everything it holds to their wallet, in the stock. Then it keeps sending.
